Description
Overview Draw walls over the project site (or your own terrain), add massing models as .glb files, and watch the wind move through it. The wind is a stack of 2D lattice Boltzmann flow slices at fixed elevations. Each slice only sees the terrain, masses and walls tall enough to reach it, so low in a canyon the walls of the canyon steer the air, while higher slices pass over the ridges. Neighbouring slices mix, so air drops into canyons and wakes, and it slows just above the ground. Particles that meet a low roof, wall or ridge rise over it. It is an approximation rather than full 3D CFD, but it shows where wind funnels, stalls and slips over.
