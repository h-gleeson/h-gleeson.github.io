Description
Overview Draw walls over a massing model and watch the wind move through it. The wind is a stack of five 2D lattice Boltzmann flow slices, from ground level up to 34 units. Each slice only sees the masses and walls tall enough to reach it, so air higher up passes straight over lower volumes, and particles that meet a low roof or wall rise over it. It is an approximation rather than full 3D CFD, but it shows where wind funnels, stalls and slips over.
