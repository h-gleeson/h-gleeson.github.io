Card workshop

A place to work out the shape, type, color, and spacing of the homepage cards.

These previews use the homepage card styles. Changes stay here as a draft in this browser and don't touch the homepage. When you like something, copy the settings at the bottom and send them back to Claude to apply.

Starting designs

The homepage uses Storybook gilt. The other designs borrow ideas from other trading card games (frames, title bars, text boxes, borders) to compare against or borrow from. Pick one to load it into the editor below, then adjust it with the sliders. They pick up the site palette.
