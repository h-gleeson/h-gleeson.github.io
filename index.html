About me

A little about the person making all this.

I’m Henry Gleeson. I grew up in Northeast Ohio, and my work brings together architecture, drawing, digital modeling, and interactive experiments.

I like a lot of stuff, but some of my favorites are forests, playing the drums, writing, and video games. I love stories. Hearing personal anecdotes always reminds me of the possibilities of life, so let's inspire each other! My contact is below, and thanks for checking out my website!
