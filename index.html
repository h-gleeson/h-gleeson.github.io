About

// SYSTEM PROFILE

I’m Henry Gleeson. I grew up in Northeast Ohio, and my work brings together architecture, drawing, digital modeling, and interactive experiments.

This archive includes academic design projects, work from my landscape design internship, and personal writing. Each project records an idea and the process of making it.
